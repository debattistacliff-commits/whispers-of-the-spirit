# Disclaimer

Please read this before using Whispers of the Spirit.

## What the app is — and is not

Whispers of the Spirit is a **radio listening, recording and audio review tool** made for
ghost-box, EVP (electronic voice phenomena) and paranormal-investigation enthusiasts.

- It is intended for **entertainment, hobby and personal research** use.
- It **does not detect, prove or communicate with spirits, ghosts or any entity.** The sounds you
  hear come from real radio broadcasts, internet radio streams, radio static, your microphone,
  test tones, or effects added by the app.
- Fragments of words heard while sweeping radio stations are normally pieces of ordinary
  broadcasts. The human brain naturally finds words and voices in noise (pareidolia).
- The Review deck's measurements (heat map, "speech-like activity", pitch line) show **where
  sound with speech-like properties is**. They cannot tell **who** is speaking, **what** is
  being said, or **where** a sound came from.
- Slowing down, clean-up and effects can **change how audio sounds** and can create artefacts.
  Always compare with the original recording, which the app never changes.
- Optional machine transcription can be wrong, especially with noise.

Any interpretation of recordings is your own. Please share findings honestly and say how they
were made.

## Your responsibilities

- **Recording people.** Laws on recording conversations differ by country. Get consent before
  recording other people, and respect their privacy when sharing recordings.
- **Radio and streams.** Listening to public broadcasts is legal in most places, but some
  countries restrict listening to or recording certain transmissions. Broadcast content belongs
  to its broadcasters: do not republish it as your own. Public KiwiSDR receivers are run by
  volunteers — use them fairly and follow each receiver's rules.
- **Locations.** Only investigate places where you are allowed to be. Do not trespass, ask the
  owner's permission, and take care in old, dark or unsafe buildings.
- **Wellbeing.** If paranormal investigation causes you fear, distress or anxiety, take a break
  and talk to someone you trust.
